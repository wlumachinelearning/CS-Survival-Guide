## 💡 Tips from Upper Years

1. **🏆 Go to as many hackathons as possible in your first 2 years**
   * *Networking & Teammates:* If you need a team, check out the **ML & AI Club's Hack Teams**!
   * *Where to find them:* Browse [Major League Hacking (MLH)](https://mlh.io/) or LinkedIn. *(Local favorite: **[case]HACKS** 👀)*
   * 💡 *Pro Tip:* Don't worry if you don't feel "ready"—hackathons are meant for learning on the go and adding concrete projects to your resume quickly.

2. **📚 Take CP363 (Databases) and CP317 (Software Engineering) ASAP in 2nd Year**
   * *Why?* A vast majority of entry-level engineering and data roles require SQL proficiency.
   * *Bonus:* CP317 forces you to build a substantial team project right before your first co-op/internship job search.

3. **💼 Work hard to get into the Co-op Program**
   * Having official co-op support drastically accelerates getting your foot in the door.
   * 💡 *Quick Tips for Co-op Admission:*
     * **Lead, don't just join:** Aim for **Executive** roles in campus clubs rather than just general membership—leadership experience stands out on applications.
     * **Maintain high grades:** Keep your GPA at a **B+ or higher** throughout your first year.

4. **🤝 Reach out to alumni on LinkedIn**
   * Send personalized connection requests asking for short 15-minute coffee chats to learn about their career paths. Alumni love helping out current students!

5. **🎯 Tailor multiple resumes for different career paths**
   * Don't blast a generic resume everywhere. Maintain tailored versions for specific roles. For instance, I like to keep 2resumes at all times (but you can keep more for your interests):
     * *Software Engineer (SWE)*
     * *Machine Learning (ML) / Data Scientist*
   * 💡 *Pro Tip:* Align keywords in your bullet points with the specific job description you're applying to if you can.

6. **🏫 Aim for TA (Teaching Assistant) or RA (Research Assistant) positions**
   * If you maintain a strong GPA, reach out to professors. Having academic or technical experience on campus holds significantly more weight on a tech resume than non-relevant service jobs.

7. **✨ Believe in yourself!**
   * Tech can feel competitive, but consistency and proactive effort always pay off. You've got this! 🚀

* 🎓 **Complete Self-Study Curriculum:** [Coding Interview University](https://github.com/jwasham/coding-interview-university) *(Comprehensive multi-month CS study plan)*

### Software Engineering & Live Debugging
Prepare for practical pairing sessions and live code-debugging rounds:
* [Real-SWE](https://www.real-swe.com/) — Real-world practical SWE interview challenges.
* [Whitebox](https://whitebox.ac/) — Live code testing and debugging scenarios.

### Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA)
* 🎯 **Goal:** Complete the **Blind 75** set at a minimum, with an ultimate goal of finishing the **NeetCode 150**.
* 🛠️ **Resources:**
  * [NeetCode.io](https://neetcode.io/) — Structured practice roadmaps with video explanations.
  * [VisuAlgo](https://visualgo.net/en) — Animated visualizer for data structures and algorithms.
  * [CS Visualized (CSVistool)](https://csvistool.com/) — Interactive visualizations of core CS algorithms and data structures.

### System Design
Learn how large-scale architecture works:
* 📖 **Guided Course:** [Codemia System Design](https://codemia.io/system-design) *(Free tier available)*
* 📂 **Open Source Guides:**
  * [System Design Primer (Donne Martin)](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer)
  * [System Design Notes](https://github.com/liquidslr/system-design-notes)

### SQL, Databases & Data Science
Essential for Data Engineering, Data Analytics, and Backend Development:
* 🏋️ [PGExercises](https://pgexercises.com/) — Interactive PostgreSQL challenges.
* ⚡ [LeetCode Database Study Plan](https://leetcode.com/problemset/database/) — Standard database interview problems.
* 📊 [HackerRank Data Science](https://www.hackerrank.com/dashboard) — Practice SQL, Python, and stats for DS roles.

### Cybersecurity, Linux & DevOps
* 🐧 **Linux Command Line:** [OverTheWire: Bandit](https://overthewire.org/wargames/bandit/) — Gamified Linux terminal challenges for beginners.
* 🚩 **Cybersecurity / CTF:** [Hack The Box](https://www.hackthebox.com/) — Hands-on penetration testing and security labs.
